## 💡 The Core Problem & Our Approach
Before writing a single prompt or line of code, our team conducted thorough **user and market research** to identify the friction points in the current internship ecosystem. 

### The Findings:
* **For Students:** The traditional process of hunting for internships, customizing resumes, and cold-applying to dozens of companies is exhausting, repetitive, and often discouraging.
* **For Companies:** Sorting through hundreds of generic CVs, filtering unqualified applicants, and managing the initial selection phase consumes massive amounts of time and HR resources.

### Our Solution: *The Internship Finds You*
We flipped the traditional model upside down. Instead of students chasing companies, our platform enables **companies to find the exact students they need**. 
* **Passive Discovery:** Students build their profiles, showcase their verifiable skills, and let companies reach out to them directly with internship offers.
* **Continuous Growth via Linker Challenges:** To keep students engaged and continuously improving, the platform features **Linker Challenges**. Students can solve real-world tasks, level up their profiles, and prove their practical skills directly to potential employers.
